# Third-party material

- `knowledge/ux-guidelines.csv`, `knowledge/app-interface.csv`,
  `knowledge/charts.csv`: from
  [nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill](https://github.com/nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill),
  MIT licence (`knowledge/LICENSE`). Searched by `design_canvas` action
  `knowledge` with `topic` ux, mobile or charts.
- `knowledge/platforms/*.md`: written for Atlas from Apple's Human Interface
  Guidelines and Google's Material 3 guidance (numbers and patterns, not their
  text).
- Photographs come from Pexels, Unsplash or Openverse and carry their own
  licences, recorded per image in `assets/credits.json`.

## Store screenshots

`design_canvas` action `references` downloads App Store screenshots through
Apple's public iTunes Search API and Google Play screenshots from the public
store pages, at the time the author asks, into the design file's `refs/`
folder as a study sheet. They belong to their publishers, are never bundled
with Atlas and are never placed in a design.

## Define and reconcile the calculation

Use data-analysis and research-evidence for actual datasets and experiments.
Define grain, metric, population, units, dates and denominators.
Preserve raw inputs and record missing, malformed, zero and excluded values
without silently treating them as interchangeable.

Check key uniqueness and join cardinality before aggregating.
Reconcile row counts and material totals after consequential transformations.
Separate supplied facts, editable assumptions and derived calculations.

## Verify the model and communicate results

Retain formulas and chart source data when editable output is requested.
Distinguish stored formulas, cached values and freshly recalculated results.
Use a compatible engine for required recalculation and independent calculations
for representative totals. Report unavailable recalculation honestly.

Inspect rendered charts for readable labels and truthful scales.
For experiments, retain assignment unit, exposure, exclusions and uncertainty;
descriptive differences alone do not establish causality.

Deliver method, source identity, derived artifacts, reconciliation evidence and
decision-relevant limitations. Avoid unsupported precision and invented data.

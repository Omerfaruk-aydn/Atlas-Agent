# Tablet (iPad Air 834 x 1194, Android tablets 800 to 1280 dp)

- A tablet is not a stretched phone. Use the width: sidebar plus content (sidebar 280 to 320 pt) or a two-pane list and detail; bottom tab bars become a sidebar or a top tab bar.
- Content columns cap near 680 pt for reading; forms and settings sit in a centred 560 to 640 pt column.
- Margins 24 to 32 pt. Touch targets stay 44 pt (iPadOS) or 48 dp (Android); pointer and keyboard are likely, so show hover and focus where it helps.
- Support both orientations in the plan; show landscape when the layout changes (split panes).
- Popovers instead of full-screen sheets for secondary choices; modals 540 to 640 pt wide.

## Observe, resolve and operate

Use desktop-automation or browser-automation for the requested surface.
Establish actual application/tab identity, account and current readable state.
Inspect supported action capabilities before choosing semantic or pixel input.
A window list, accessible tree and screenshot establish different properties.

Batch deterministic steps within a grounded boundary. Re-observe after navigation,
focus changes, resize or stale-target errors when the next action depends on new
state. Do not replay an unchanged failing action or silently substitute a website
for a requested desktop application.

## Recover and prove the outcome

Preserve the raw consequential error and distinguish observed state from suspected
cause. On uncertain input outcomes, inspect before retrying a write.
Use legitimate protected credential and authentication handoff mechanisms.

Verify business identity and final state: the correct track and active playback,
the intended profile and level, the saved setting or the created object.
Successful clicks alone do not complete the assignment.

Use tool_pipeline only with known prerequisites and normal controls.
Report elapsed/provider/tool timing separately only when instrumented evidence
supports those intervals. Keep cancellation responsive and release owned sessions.
